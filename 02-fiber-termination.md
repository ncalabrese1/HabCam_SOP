# Fiber Optic Termination (Clevis & Brooming)

### Purpose

Underwater fiber optic cables are used in environments where standard communication cables cannot provide the required durability, data capacity, or long-term reliability[cite: 8]. On the *Henry B. Bigelow*, the fiber optic cable run through the winch wire is cut annually, requiring a new fiber optic termination prior to survey deployment[cite: 8].

<br>

### Termination Workflow

#### Part 1: Inserting the Fiber Optic into the Clevis
1. Mark and cut ~10m (33ft) off the ship's fiber optic cable using a grinder and cutting wheel to discard stressed wire[cite: 8]. Place a hose clamp past the 33ft mark[cite: 8].
2. Measure and mark 24ft from the open end, placing another hose clamp[cite: 8].
3. Score outer shield wires with a Dremel, peel back armor wires to the score mark, and expose black inner jacketing[cite: 8].
4. Insert wire through the narrow end of the sandblasted clevis[cite: 8].

#### Part 2: Creating the Steel Broom

1. Fan armor wires out at 90° angles around the hose clamp to form a "broom"[cite: 8].
2. Walk clevis out to the aft deck, rig a come-along winch to deck padeyes, and draw 4–6 inches of the broom firmly into the clevis socket well[cite: 8].

#### Part 3: Epoxy Pouring
1. Wrap electrical/splicing tape around clevis top to build a 2–3 inch reservoir[cite: 8].
2. Mix Socketfast resin and catalyst thoroughly for 2 minutes[cite: 8].
3. Pour mixture into clevis well, tapping sides to release air bubbles[cite: 8]. Allow 24 hours room-temperature cure (or 30 mins at 180–200°F with heat guns)[cite: 8].

<br>

### J-Box Integration & ST Connectorization

#### Part 4: The Boot & Fairing
1. Wrap 6 rolls of 2-inch self-vulcanizing tape to form a 2.5ft tapered boot from the clevis base[cite: 8].
2. Apply 18-inch heavy zip ties along the tow cable for 15–20 feet as fairing to eliminate cable vibration[cite: 8].

#### Part 5: J-Box Wiring & Oil Fill
1. Route power and fiber wires into J-box port, clamp internally with a hose clamp, and attach 12-10 AWG ring terminals to power wires[cite: 8].
2. Splice and polish singlemode fiber strands using 3M Hot Melt ST connectors (15-20 figure-8 passes on 9-micron gray sheet followed by green/white lapping films)[cite: 8]. Inspect under 200x scope for core chips or scratches[cite: 8].
3. Seal yellow urethane bladder, torque 5mm Allen screws evenly, and fill J-box completely with compensating oil using a vacuum pump[cite: 8].

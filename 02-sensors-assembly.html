Detailed Subsystem Preparation & Assembly Instructions

BlueView Forward Looking Sonar (1x)

Preparation & Mounting Steps: 1. Prior to attaching BlueView to the front of the vehicle, ensure the bracket has rubber gaskets glued to them[cite: 8]. 2. Attach BlueView to the bracket, with the Impulse connector facing towards the back of the vehicle[cite: 8]. 3. Thread four stainless steel screws through the bracket[cite: 8]. 4. Apply Aqua Shield over all threads prior to adding washers and nuts[cite: 8]. 5. Add stainless steel washers, lock washers, and nuts, then tighten securely with 9/16” ratcheting wrenches[cite: 8].


Main Electronics Bottle - WHOI Engineered (1x)

Maintenance & Assembly Highlights: 1. Ensure vehicle power is completely off before removing[cite: 8]. 2. Disconnect all external bulkhead cables[cite: 8]. 3. Remove main bottle top clamps using a 9/16” ratcheting wrench and carefully transfer bottle onto its dedicated bench cradle[cite: 8]. 4. Remove the 4 outer threaded rods using a 7/16” wrench and pop the vacuum purge plug with a 3/16” hex key to release internal vacuum[cite: 8]. 5. Inspect internal fuses, replace desiccant packs (4 large packs), inspect and re-grease endcap O-rings with Molykote 55, and re-pull 16 inHg vacuum for 24 hours to verify stability[cite: 8].

# Fiber Optics Pull Test Protocol

### Purpose

A pull test (proof load test) using a water bag on a hydraulic winch line is performed to safely verify that the winch, termination clevis, and mounting structure can handle their maximum rated weight capacity without slippage or epoxy failure[cite: 8].

### Test Execution & Verification

1. Coordinate with ship's Chief Bosun to suspend the Coast Guard water bag over deck[cite: 8].
2. Engage pumps and fill water bag, monitoring the digital load cell readout[cite: 8].
3. Slow pumps at 9,000 lbs tension and stop pumps at **10,000 lbs** target tension[cite: 8].
4. Hold 10,000 lbs proof load for 1 full minute[cite: 8].

5. Drain water bag and inspect reference hose clamp above clevis[cite: 8]. Verify **zero clevis slippage** occurred along the cable jacket[cite: 8].
6. Re-connect J-box telemetry and test optical continuity to HabCam control stations[cite: 8].

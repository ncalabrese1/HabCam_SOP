# Marine Development & Advanced Technology Program
*(Formerly Advanced Technology Program)*[cite: 1]

**Population & Ecosystems Monitoring & Analysis Division • Ecosystems Surveys Branch**[cite: 1]

---

> ### Program Mission
> **To develop surveys, technologies, and analytical approaches to inform stock and ecosystem assessments across the Northeast region.**[cite: 1]

---

## Addressing Marine Development & Survey Challenges

Offshore wind energy development in the U.S. Northeast region interacts with **14 long-term scientific surveys**[cite: 1]. These developments introduce significant survey challenges, including physical preclusion from traditional sampling areas, impacts on statistical survey designs, habitat changes, and operational sampling constraints[cite: 1].

### 6-Step Framework for Survey Mitigation & Adaptation:
1. **Evaluate Designs & Impacts**[cite: 1]
2. **Design New Methods**[cite: 1]
3. **Calibrate New & Existing Surveys**[cite: 1]
4. **Bridge Solutions**[cite: 1]
5. **Conduct New Surveys**[cite: 1]
6. **Communications & New Data**[cite: 1]

## Core Technologies & Research Initiatives

### 1. Habitat Camera (HabCam) Survey Operations
*Jointly managed by the Program and the Shellfish Survey Group.*[cite: 1]

* **Lead Operations:** Responsible for survey scheduling, vessel logistics, vehicle maintenance, electronics, camera calibrations, database architecture, and assessment analytics[cite: 1].
* **Ocean Acidification Monitoring:** In 2024, a channelized optical system was integrated into HabCam to characterize ocean chemistry alongside benthic surveys[cite: 1].
* **Scallop Assessment:** Directly couples sea scallop biomass estimates with bottom water DIC and $p\text{CO}_2$ data to evaluate ocean chemistry impacts on scallop distribution[cite: 1].

### 2. Long-Range Autonomous Underwater Vehicles (LRAUV)
*Designed by MBARI; modified for fisheries science.*[cite: 1]

<p naming="lrauv-image">
  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/06019c5f-e82d-478c-8e63-da5d24a1bd91" width="100%" max-width="600" style="border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid #cbd5e1;" alt="Long Range AUV Deployment" />
  <br><em>Figure 2: Long-Range AUV (LRAUV) equipped with optical imaging payloads for autonomous survey operations.</em>
</p>

* **Wind Farm Access:** Autonomous underwater vehicles can follow target depths or oceanographic features for days/weeks, enabling survey access inside wind energy areas while reducing vessel days at sea[cite: 1].
* **Stereo Camera Payloads:** NEFSC and WHOI equipped two payloads with stereo cameras to replicate the HabCam imaging setup[cite: 1].
* **Field Deployments:** Sampled over 200 nautical miles during scallop surveys and completed nearshore deployments around South Fork Wind Farm[cite: 1].
* **Calibration Studies:** Active research evaluating catchability differences between AUV, HabCam, and traditional dredge gear[cite: 1].

### 3. Active Acoustics & Uncrewed Surface Vehicles (DriX USV)
*Safely sampling around offshore turbine structures.*[cite: 1]

<p naming="drix-image">
  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/43316965-20e2-40a4-960b-05b1ebe6050a" width="100%" max-width="600" style="border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid #cbd5e1;" alt="DriX Uncrewed Surface Vehicle" />
  <br><em>Figure 3: DriX Uncrewed Surface Vehicle (USV) deployed for acoustic sampling in offshore wind areas.</em>
</p>

* **24-Hour Continuous Operations:** Uncrewed Surface Vehicles (USVs) carry out continuous active acoustic sampling within 20–30 meters of wind turbine structures where crewed ships face navigational risks[cite: 1].
* **Offshore Missions:** Deployed the DriX18 USV across offshore wind areas including Block Island, Cox Ledge, and Vineyard Wind[cite: 1].
* **Forage Habitat Research:** Acoustic survey designs evaluate prey field dynamics and North Atlantic Right Whale forage habitats[cite: 1].
* **Cooperative Acoustics:** Partnering with industry to assess fish life history, infer catchability, and study species like Atlantic mackerel[cite: 1].

### 4. Machine Learning & Artificial Intelligence for Optical Data
*Transitioning from manual image processing to automated computer vision.*[cite: 1]

* **Scalable Analytics:** Developing automated uncertainty quantification and extrapolation models for regional stock abundance assessments[cite: 1].

## Future Strategic Directions

* **Passive Acoustics Surveys:** Developing fisheries-independent passive acoustic monitoring for groundfish such as Atlantic cod and haddock[cite: 1].
* **AUV & eDNA Integration:** Expanding AUV capabilities by combining optical imaging payloads with environmental DNA (eDNA) sampling[cite: 1].
* **Model-Based Data Integration:** Integrating traditional survey data with advanced technology datasets using advanced geostatistical models[cite: 1].
* **Catchability Studies:** Conducting directed field studies to evaluate gear efficiency and catchability across gear types[cite: 1].

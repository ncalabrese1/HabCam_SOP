# Marine Development & Advanced Technology Program

**Population & Ecosystems Monitoring & Analysis Division • Ecosystems Surveys Branch**

---

> ### Program Mission
> **To develop surveys, technologies, and analytical approaches to inform stock and ecosystem assessments across the Northeast region.**

---

## Addressing Marine Development & Survey Challenges

Offshore wind energy development in the U.S. Northeast region interacts with **14 long-term scientific surveys**. These developments introduce significant survey challenges, including physical preclusion from traditional sampling areas, impacts on statistical survey designs, habitat changes, and operational sampling constraints.

### 6-Step Framework for Survey Mitigation & Adaptation:
1. **Evaluate Designs & Impacts**
2. **Design New Methods**
3. **Calibrate New & Existing Surveys**
4. **Bridge Solutions**
5. **Conduct New Surveys**
6. **Communications & New Data**

## Core Technologies & Research Initiatives

### 1. Habitat Camera (HabCam) Survey Operations
*Jointly managed by the Program and the Shellfish Survey Group.*

* **Lead Operations:** Responsible for survey scheduling, vessel logistics, vehicle maintenance, electronics, camera calibrations, database architecture, and assessment analytics.
* **Ocean Acidification Monitoring:** In 2024, a channelized optical system was integrated into HabCam to characterize ocean chemistry alongside benthic surveys.
* **Scallop Assessment:** Directly couples sea scallop biomass estimates with bottom water DIC and $p\text{CO}_2$ data to evaluate ocean chemistry impacts on scallop distribution.

### 2. Long-Range Autonomous Underwater Vehicles (LRAUV)
*Designed by MBARI; modified for fisheries science.*

<p naming="lrauv-image">
  <img src="YOUR_LRAUV_RED_SUBMARINE_IMAGE_URL_HERE" width="100%" max-width="600" style="border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid #cbd5e1;" alt="Red Long Range AUV" />
  <br><em>Figure 2: Long-Range AUV (LRAUV) equipped with stereo optical camera payloads for autonomous survey operations.</em>
</p>

* **Wind Farm Access:** Autonomous underwater vehicles can follow target depths or oceanographic features for days/weeks, enabling survey access inside wind energy areas while reducing vessel days at sea.
* **Stereo Camera Payloads:** NEFSC and WHOI equipped two payloads with stereo cameras to replicate the HabCam imaging setup.
* **Field Deployments:** Sampled over 200 nautical miles during scallop surveys and completed nearshore deployments around South Fork Wind Farm.
* **Calibration Studies:** Active research evaluating catchability differences between AUV, HabCam, and traditional dredge gear.

* **Offshore Missions:** Deployed the DriX18 USV across offshore wind areas including Block Island, Cox Ledge, and Vineyard Wind.
* **Forage Habitat Research:** Acoustic survey designs evaluate prey field dynamics and North Atlantic Right Whale forage habitats.
* **Cooperative Acoustics:** Partnering with industry to assess fish life history, infer catchability, and study species like Atlantic mackerel.

* **Scalable Analytics:** Developing automated uncertainty quantification and extrapolation models for regional stock abundance assessments.

## Future Strategic Directions

* **Passive Acoustics Surveys:** Developing fisheries-independent passive acoustic monitoring for groundfish such as Atlantic cod and haddock.
* **AUV & eDNA Integration:** Expanding AUV capabilities by combining optical imaging payloads with environmental DNA (eDNA) sampling.
* **Model-Based Data Integration:** Integrating traditional survey data with advanced technology datasets using advanced geostatistical models.
* **Catchability Studies:** Conducting directed field studies to evaluate gear efficiency and catchability across gear types.
